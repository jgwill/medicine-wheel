## The definition

A workspace is the place where work was done: a location. It holds the things the work left there (files, sessions, charts, notes), each at its own place. Returning to a workspace means arriving at that location and finding them where they were left.

A workspace is not a configuration, a server, a store or a session. Those can be at a location, and the location is what the word names.

## What this changes in the wheel's vocabulary

- "Rehydrating a workspace" means returning to the location, not restoring a running state. What comes back at a delay's date is the follow-up prompt, potentially with its session (rispec `rispecs/delayed-resolution.spec.md`).
- A location can be navigated: things in it have positions relative to one another. William places a navigable, possibly three-dimensional view of it in a later phase.
