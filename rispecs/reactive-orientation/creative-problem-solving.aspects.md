# Creative Problem-Solving Aspects

## Relational Problem Framing
Redefine problems through kinship lens. Rather than isolated technical issues, surface tensions within system relations. Platform maps stakeholder interdependence to reveal hidden leverage points.

## Emergent Solution Pathways
Allow answers to unfold rather than forcing predetermined paths. System holds creative tension between current state and desired future, inviting natural resolution.

## Structural Accountability Integration
Every solution traces back to community impact. Platform embeds inquiry-weave linkage, ensuring problem-solving serves relational wholeness, not extraction.

## Iterative Wisdom Refinement
Solutions spiral, not conclude. Each cycle surfaces new questions. Medicine-wheel rhythm prevents premature closure, honoring process over destination.
