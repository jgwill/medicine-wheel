# Brainstorming Aspects

## Divergent Ideation
Generate multiple solution pathways without premature filtering. Platform surfaces competing ideas in real-time, enabling team exploration of design space. Consensus emerges from witnessed possibility.

## Collective Intelligence Amplification
Weave individual insights into shared understanding. System honors each voice while surfacing patterns across perspectives. Wisdom lives in relation, not isolation.

## Structured Permission Holding
Create psychological safety for tentative thinking. Platform gates judgment until ideation completes. Scaffolding ensures ideas mature before critique.

## Novelty Within Tradition
Honor existing patterns while inviting emergence. Brainstorming integrates medicine-wheel ceremony with modern ideation, creating culturally grounded innovation.
